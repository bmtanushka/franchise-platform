Compliance & Ethics

Foundational to everything
we do

Compliance and ethics are foundational to the Luna Verde 5 model. All services are structured to align with federal and state regulations, including RESPA, licensing laws, and consumer protection standards.

Franchisees are trained to operate as facilitators and referral partners — not unlicensed brokers — using approved workflows, disclosures, and documentation.

Ethical conduct, transparency, and client-first decision-making are mandatory expectations across the entire network.

  • Aligned with federal and state regulations including RESPA and consumer protection standards.
  • Franchisees trained as facilitators and referral partners, not unlicensed brokers.
  • Approved workflows, disclosures, and documentation required at every step.
  • Ethical conduct and transparency are mandatory expectations network-wide.
  • Client-first decision-making is built into every process and interaction.
Licensing & Disclosures

Operating within full
compliance

Luna Verde 5 operates through a combination of licensed professionals, broker-of-record relationships, and third-party lending and real estate partners. Not all services are available in all states, and licensing requirements vary by jurisdiction.

Franchisees are required to follow state-specific licensing rules, provide appropriate disclosures, and operate only within the scope permitted by their credentials and agreements.

Full licensing and disclosure information is provided to clients prior to engagement.
