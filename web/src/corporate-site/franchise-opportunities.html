Franchise Overview

A model built for scalable
entrepreneurship

The Luna Verde Corporate franchise is built for entrepreneurs who want multiple income streams without building separate businesses. Franchisees operate within a structured system that prioritizes compliance, automation, and scalability.

The model allows participation at different levels — from referral-focused roles to fully engaged operators. Support, systems, and training are centralized, allowing franchisees to focus on growth.

This path is designed to align with different time commitments, experience levels, and income goals. Franchisees focus on relationship building and introductions, as well as operations and learning.

Earnings & Economics

Diversified income across multiple pillars

Franchise earnings are driven by multiple service pillars rather than a single commission source. This diversification helps stabilize income and reduce reliance on any one market.

Compensation is structured around completed milestones and compliant referral outcomes — not employment or hourly work. Clear rules and reporting ensure transparency for all parties.

  • Earnings tied to completed, compliant outcomes — not hours worked.
  • Multi-pillar model reduces dependence on any single service category.
  • Transparent reporting and clear compensation rules across the network.
  • Income potential scales with engagement level and pillar participation.
