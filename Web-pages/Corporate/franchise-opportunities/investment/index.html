Initial Franchise Investment Breakdown

Investing in a Luna Verde Corporate franchise requires an initial franchise fee of $3,900. This fee covers a wide range of expenses that ensure you're ready to open and operate your franchise with confidence, including:

  • Initial Franchise Training — Gain comprehensive knowledge of how to run your business, work with clients, and implement our proven business model.
  • Materials and Resources — Receive all the materials and resources you'll need to launch your business from day one.
  • Ongoing Franchise Support — Enjoy continuous guidance and support from the Luna Verde Corporate team and the extensive United Franchise Group network.
Financial Requirements: Available liquid capital of $10,000 is required to qualify as a Luna Verde Corporate franchise owner.
